# Daikibo Project: 10 Questions and Answers

## 1. What was the main purpose of analyzing downtime by factory and device type?

To identify which factory had the most downtime and which device types contributed most.

## 2. How many Daikibo factory locations are included in the telemetry analysis?

Four factory locations.

## 3. What does the calculated Unhealthy field represent?

Ten minutes of potential downtime for each unhealthy status message, matching the reporting interval.

## 4. Which factory has the highest downtime total in the supplied dashboard?

Daikibo Factory Seiko.

## 5. Which device type has the largest total in the displayed device chart?

LaserWelder.

## 6. Why is the Downtime Per Factory chart configured as a dashboard filter?

Selecting a factory filters the device-type chart to show downtime for that factory.

## 7. What insight is the classification of Equality Scores intended to provide?

Which roles and locations have more significant pay-equality concerns.

## 8. What equality score represents ideal gender pay equality?

Zero.

## 9. How should scores of -9 and +10 be classified?

Both are **Fair**, because their absolute values are no greater than 10.

## 10. How should scores of -30 and +15 be classified?

- -30 is **Highly Discriminative** because its absolute value is greater than 20.
- +15 is **Unfair** because its absolute value is greater than 10 and no greater than 20.
